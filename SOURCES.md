# Data sources

## Mrk 42
Izviekova, I. O., Vavilova, I. B., Kompaniiets, O. V., Zamora, O., & Clavero, R. (2026).
*The 2MIG isolated AGNs. 3. Optical–IR variability and dust reverberation in the NLSy1 galaxies Mrk 42 and Mrk 493.*
arXiv:2605.06517.

Reference-star V magnitudes: APASS DR9. Candidate stars were cross-checked against Gaia DR3 and VSX as described in the paper.

## IC 5287 and Mrk 845
Izviekova, I. O., Vavilova, I. B., Kompaniiets, O. V., Zamora, O., & Clavero, R. (2026).
*The 2MIG isolated AGNs – 5. Hot-dust reverberation and spectroscopic constraints on BLR–dust scales in IC 5287 and Mrk 845.*
Manuscript.

Reference-star coordinates and V magnitudes: APASS DR9, with field-specific stability and seeing checks described in the manuscript.
