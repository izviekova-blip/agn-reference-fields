IC 1495 was monitored on two consecutive nights in 2025 October: 26 exposures of 250 s in Johnson/Bessell R on October 13 and 35 exposures of 300 s in Johnson/Bessell V on October 14.

Reference-star use. Three numbered field stars were used for differential photometry. The working values adopted in the reductions are R = 13.994, 15.374, 13.489 and V = 14.544, 15.816, 13.876 for stars 1–3, respectively.
Current coordinate status. The supplied finding chart and working photometry identify the three comparison stars unambiguously in the CAMELOT2 field. Their catalogue J2000 coordinates and magnitude uncertainties are not yet stored in this repository, so the site leaves those columns blank instead of inferring positions from detector pixels.
