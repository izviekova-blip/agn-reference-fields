What this resource contains

AGN finding charts, local comparison-star sequences, reference-star J2000 coordinates, APASS DR9 V-band photometry, IAC80/CAMELOT2 observing information, and machine-readable CSV/JSON tables. Current fields include Mrk 42, IC 5287, and Mrk 845.
