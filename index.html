What this resource contains

AGN finding charts, local comparison-star sequences, IAC80/CAMELOT2 observing information, and machine-readable reference tables. Mrk 42, IC 5287 and Mrk 845 include APASS DR9 multiband catalogue photometry with J2000 comparison-star coordinates. IC 1495 currently includes the supplied numbered finding chart and the R/V working comparison-star magnitudes used in the IAC80 reductions; its comparison-star catalogue coordinates will be added after the original coordinate table is imported.
